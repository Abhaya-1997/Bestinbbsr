💰 The Daily Boarding Rate Rule in Bhubaneswar

Average Daily Boarding Rates (2026)
₹400 – ₹900 per day
Standard non-AC boarding with basic home-cooked meals ranges from ₹400–₹500. Premium AC facilities with 24/7 CCTV, branded kibble, and supervised play areas cost between ₹700–₹900+ per day.

Long-term stays (over 7 days) usually attract a 10-15% discount. Always confirm if the daily rate includes food or if you are required to bring your pet's regular kibble.

📍 5 Best Dog Daycare & Boarding Centers in Bhubaneswar

1
Petzoo
Patia / Nandan Vihar · Premium Facility
Best Amenities

Petzoo operates a massive 3,200 sq ft gated facility tailored for complete pet comfort. They are highly professional and offer "staycation" packages for dogs.

Rates & Facilities: Daycare starts at ₹649 for 9 hours. Full 24-hour AC boarding starts at ₹899/day.

Diet & Perks: Includes 2 to 3 meals a day, basic vet check-ups, and grooming. They offer 24/7 CCTV surveillance and maintain a strict tick-free environment.

🩺 Crucial Rules: Vaccinations, Diet & Vet Protocols

Before dropping your pet off at any boarding facility in Bhubaneswar, you must prepare for the following standard operating procedures:

  1. Up-to-Date Vaccinations: No verified kennel will accept a dog without an updated vaccination card. You must have current records for Anti-Rabies, DHPPi (Parvo/Distemper), and Kennel Cough.
  2. Tick & Flea Prevention: Ensure your dog has a recent tick treatment (like a Bravecto pill or Spot-on). Facilities like Petzoo enforce a strict tick-free environment to protect all boarders.
  3. Emergency Veterinary Access: Always ask the boarding center about their on-call vet protocol. Premium centers have a 24/7 vet on backup, while others may ask you for your regular vet's emergency number.
  4. Dietary Transitions: If your dog eats a specific brand of kibble or requires a specialized diet, provide your own food. Sudden diet changes in a new environment often lead to severe stomach upsets for dogs.
